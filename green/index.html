What we do

Three core services, done with precision, passion and attention to detail, using professional-grade products and proven processes.

Auto detailing

Complete vehicle care, inside and out, taking every area to a higher level of cleanliness, appearance and presentation.

Includes a professional hand wash, wheels and tires, windows, a complete vacuum, seats and carpets, stain removal, air vents, the trunk and more.

A note from Julio

At Aston Maverick, we are dedicated to the care, restoration and protection of vehicles, providing professional auto detailing, paint polishing & correction, and ceramic coating.

Our commitment is to care for every vehicle with precision, passion and attention to detail. We use professional-grade products and proven processes to deliver results that exceed our customers’ expectations.

It’s not simply about cleaning a vehicle. It’s about restoring its appearance, enhancing its beauty and protecting its value.

We believe a vehicle is much more than a means of transportation. It’s part of our customers’ lives, their stories and their accomplishments. Our purpose is to care for, transform, restore and protect every vehicle, combining experience, technology and a genuine passion for detail.
